Provenance of the raw source art in this directory.

grass_dirt.png
  Original: "128x64 Grass A to Dirt A.png" from the "Autotiles" update pack
            of "1000+ Isometric Floor Tiles" (Screaming Brain Studios, CC0).
  Format:   512x448 RGB, a 4x7 grid of 128x64 isometric cells.
  Used by:  tools/pack_terrain_atlas.py -- grass = cell (col 1, row 3),
            dirt = cell (col 1, row 4).

water.png
  Original: "Grass A - Water Flat 128x64.png" from the "Water" update pack
            of the same CC0 set.
  Format:   1024x384 RGB, an 8x6 grid of 128x64 isometric cells.
  Used by:  tools/pack_terrain_atlas.py -- the most water-dominant cell is
            selected deterministically and brightness-ramped into 4 anim frames.

Only the specific sheets consumed by the packer are vendored here (not the full
multi-megabyte pack). To regenerate assets/tilesets/terrain_atlas.png from these
sources:  python3 tools/pack_terrain_atlas.py

aerial_grass_rock.png
  Original: "aerial_grass_rock_diff_2k.png" from Poly Haven (CC0).
  Format:   2048x2048 RGB, seamless top-down aerial grass/soil diffuse.
  Used by:  tools/pack_terrain_atlas.py -- the HD ground mega-texture (#247).

rock_boulder_dry.png
  Original: "rock_boulder_dry_diff_2k.png" from Poly Haven (CC0),
            https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry.
  Format:   2048x2048 RGB, warm-grey dry sandstone/boulder diffuse (sat ~17%).
  Used by:  tools/gen_cliffs.py -- high-passed to warm-grey rock grain and
            composited under the cliff-face palette gradient (#248 Cliffs v2).
